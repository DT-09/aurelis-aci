SECURITY CENTER

Designed for inspectability.

Identity

Google OAuth for authorized admins; invitation-based customer access; server-side sessions.

Isolation

Customer tenant scope is derived from the authenticated session. Client-supplied tenant IDs are not trusted.

Evidence

Decisions and actions produce traceable evidence with integrity metadata.

Least privilege

Capabilities and API scopes are designed to be explicit, revocable and auditable.

Honest scope

Aurelis does not claim certifications or integrations that have not been implemented and tested.

Operational controls

Audit logs, secure cookies, rate limiting and reauthentication should be enabled and verified in production.
